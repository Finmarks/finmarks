# Trademarks

> This page explains the project's position. It is not legal advice. If your use is commercially significant, ask a lawyer.

## The short version

The MIT licence covers **this repository's code, schemas and metadata**. It does **not** cover the logos.

Every mark distributed here belongs to its owner. HDFC Bank owns the HDFC Bank logo; PhonePe owns the PhonePe logo. Nothing about this repository transfers, dilutes, or licenses those rights, and no contributor here has the standing to grant you permission to use them.

## What that means in practice

Reproducing a logo to **identify the thing it names** is normally fine. That is nominative use, and it is why a comparison table can show competitor logos and why your app can show a bank's mark next to a linked account.

Typical safe uses:

- Showing a bank's logo beside a user's linked account
- A payment method picker displaying UPI app icons
- An account aggregator consent screen naming the FIP
- Documentation or a comparison table referring to real providers

Typical unsafe uses:

- Putting a bank's logo on your marketing site in a way that implies partnership
- Using a mark in your own logo, product name, app icon, or domain
- Suggesting endorsement, affiliation, or certification you do not have
- Modifying a mark — recolouring, distorting, adding to it — and continuing to present it as the brand's

The line is **confusion**. If a reasonable person could conclude the brand endorses you, you are on the wrong side of it, regardless of what any licence says.

Some brands publish explicit rules — minimum clear space, approved colourways, prohibited treatments. Where they do, those rules govern. Check the brand's own guidelines before shipping.

## Why the logos are here at all

An interoperable financial system needs its participants to be identifiable. A UPI app that cannot show you which bank you are paying is a worse and less safe product. These marks are included to make software correct and legible, not to trade on their goodwill.

That is also why this project holds a strict sourcing rule: assets come from official brand kits or official properties, never from third-party aggregators and never traced by hand. A distorted logo misrepresents the brand, which serves nobody.

## For contributors

By submitting a logo you confirm:

- It came from an official source, which you have declared in the PR
- You are not aware of brand guidelines that forbid its redistribution
- You claim no ownership of the mark

If a brand kit explicitly forbids redistribution, **do not submit the asset**. Open an issue instead — the entity's metadata is still valuable, and the record can carry a `null` logo indefinitely.

## For brand owners

If you own a mark in this dataset and want it changed or removed, open an issue titled `trademark: <brand>`, or email the maintainers. Requests are honoured — we will remove the asset without arguing the merits.

Two things worth knowing before you ask:

**We would rather host your correct logo than have developers use a wrong one.** Removal does not stop your mark appearing in Indian fintech products; it just means the version in circulation is a stale screenshot someone scraped. If your objection is to a specific outdated or distorted asset, sending us the current one solves the problem better than removal does.

**Metadata is separate from artwork.** We can drop the SVG and keep the factual record — name, category, IFSC prefix, website — which is public information. Tell us which you want.
